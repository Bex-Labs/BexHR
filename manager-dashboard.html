Suggested operating flow

From review to the right next action

  1. 1
    ReviewStart on Dashboard and identify the team activity that needs your attention.
  2. 2
    Check authorityConfirm whether the request is yours to decide, delegated to you, or visible for awareness only.
  3. 3
    Act or planComplete the available decision or use the schedule to plan operational coverage.
  4. 4
    Follow throughReview history and employee records, then follow up with the responsible Manager or HR where needed.

Not sure where a task belongs?

Open Dashboard first. Your coverage and decision-authority indicators will point you to the correct workspace.

Workspace access follows your current reporting-line and delegated authority.
