This returns the selected company to a new operational BexHR workspace while keeping the company and its employees.
The following are kept:
  • Company identity and Company ID
  • Employees and Employee IDs
  • Required profile and login linkage
Operational records are reset, including:
  • Payroll, salary setup, payslips and deductions
  • Leave requests, balances and manager delegations
  • Employee bank details and supplementary records
  • Reporting assignments and organisation structure
  • Email operational setup/history and workspace configuration
